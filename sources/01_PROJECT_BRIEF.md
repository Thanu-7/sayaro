# Thermal Scientific Instruments (TSI) --- Website Project Brief

## 1. Project overview

**Client:** Thermal Scientific Instruments (TSI)\
**Deliverable:** Premium, responsive, single-page corporate website\
**Primary goal:** Present TSI as a credible laboratory-equipment and
scientific-instrumentation partner, make its product range easy to
understand, and convert relevant visitors into qualified enquiries.

The website should do more than display a catalogue. It should explain
what TSI offers, communicate how the company works, establish trust
using verifiable evidence, and make it simple for a laboratory,
institution, or industrial buyer to discuss a requirement.

3.  **Thermal & Industrial Processing Equipment**

    -   Industrial drying oven

4.  **Laboratory Safety & Airflow Systems**
    -   Wall-mounted and tabletop water distillation units
    -   Electric Bunsen burner

    -   Horizontal and vertical laminar flow cabinets
5.  **Thermal Bath & Agitation Systems**

    -   Curing bath
    -   Refrigerated water bath
    -   Oil bath
    -   Circular and rectangular water baths

The brochure also contains product-specific descriptions, including
examples such as muffle furnaces up to 1000°C, 1200°C and 1400°C, and
several water-bath configurations. Use the exact model-level information
only after checking the brochure page and confirming the current product
specification with TSI.

### Do not publish without client confirmation

The following claims have appeared in earlier design concepts or draft
copy, but must be verified before going live:

-   "10+ years of expertise"
-   "500+ projects delivered"
-   "100+ clients"
-   "ISO 9001:2015 certified"
-   Named customer logos or testimonials
-   "Manufacturer", "in-house manufacturing", "custom manufacturing" or
    "turnkey laboratory solutions"
-   Installation, commissioning, AMC, service coverage, delivery
    timelines or warranty promises
-   Any statement that a product is certified, compliant, validated or
    suitable for a regulated use

A design mockup may contain placeholder figures and logos. Those are not
evidence. Remove placeholders or replace them with approved, documented
information.

## 3. Audience

The likely audiences to validate with TSI include:

-   Research and academic laboratories
-   Pharmaceutical and healthcare laboratories
-   Industrial testing and quality-control laboratories
-   Educational institutions
-   Laboratory planners, procurement teams and technical decision-makers

The website should help a visitor answer:

-   Does TSI offer the equipment I need?
-   Which product family should I enquire about?
-   Can TSI discuss my capacity, dimensions, temperature range or
    application?
-   What support is available before and after purchase?
-   How do I contact the company and share my requirement?

## 4. Recommended positioning

### Proposed positioning statement

**Your Laboratory. Engineered End to End.**

Supporting statement:

"Explore scientific and laboratory equipment from Thermal Scientific
Instruments. From sterilization and incubation to thermal processing,
airflow systems and temperature-controlled baths, TSI's product range
supports a variety of laboratory and industrial applications."

This is a positioning direction, not a claim that TSI currently delivers
complete turnkey laboratory projects. Use "end to end" only if TSI
confirms responsibility for the relevant stages. Otherwise use the safer
alternative:

**Precision Equipment for Laboratory Applications.**

### Strategic direction

Present TSI as a technically capable, requirement-led partner rather
than an undifferentiated list of products. Make product families easy to
navigate, provide useful specification information, and show genuine
evidence of engineering, quality, delivery and support.

Do not claim that TSI is uniquely capable of customization or complete
laboratory delivery until the client confirms the scope and can provide
proof.

## 6. Website success criteria

-   Visitors can identify the five product families quickly.
-   Product cards lead to useful product details or enquiry actions.
-   The company's actual capabilities are clearly explained.
-   Trust claims are supported by approved evidence.
-   The contact/enquiry route is obvious on desktop and mobile.
-   The page loads quickly, works accessibly, and is easy for TSI to
    update.
-   Enquiries capture enough information for the sales team to respond
    meaningfully.
