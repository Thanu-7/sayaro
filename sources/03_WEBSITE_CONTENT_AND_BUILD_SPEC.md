# TSI --- Single-Page Website Content & Build Specification

## 1. Website objective

Create a premium, product-led corporate website for Thermal Scientific
Instruments. The page should make the product range understandable,
communicate verified company capabilities, and turn visitors into
qualified product enquiries.

**Primary conversion:** Request a quote / Discuss a requirement\
**Secondary conversion:** Explore product categories / Download product
information / Call or email TSI

## 2. Information architecture

Recommended one-page sequence:

1.  Navigation
2.  Hero
3.  Trust strip (verified facts only)
4.  Product categories
5.  Product range / featured products
6.  Capabilities and services
7.  How the enquiry process works
8.  Quality and proof
9.  Projects / client proof (only if supplied)
10. FAQ
11. Enquiry CTA and form
12. Footer

Use anchor links for navigation: `#home`, `#products`, `#capabilities`,
`#process`, `#quality`, `#contact`.

  Field                              Type             Required?
  ---------------------------------- ---------------- -----------
  Full name                          Text             Yes
  Organization                       Text             Optional
  Work email                         Email            Yes
  Phone                              Tel              Yes
  Product category                   Select           Yes
  Product/model                      Text or select   Optional
  Application / intended use         Textarea         Yes
  Required capacity / chamber size   Text             Optional
  Operating temperature / range      Text             Optional
  Quantity                           Number or text   Optional
  Installation location              Text             Optional
  Additional details                 Textarea         Optional
  Specification/tender file          Upload           Optional

**Form behavior** - Product CTA should preselect the relevant
category/product. - Show conditional technical fields when relevant (for
example, temperature range for ovens/furnaces/incubators and
capacity/chamber size for autoclaves). - Validate email and phone. -
Show a clear success state after submission. - Send the enquiry to a
client-approved destination. - Include privacy/consent text and link to
a privacy policy. - Do not promise a response time unless the client
confirms one.

## 5. UX and visual design requirements

### Layout

-   Maximum content width around 1200--1320px.
-   Generous vertical spacing.
-   Strong editorial headline hierarchy.
-   Product cards should be image-led but still provide useful text.
-   Keep sections visually distinct without excessive background colors.
-   Sticky or compact navigation is acceptable if it does not obstruct
    content.

### Responsive behavior

-   Desktop: 5-column category grid only if cards remain readable;
    otherwise use 3 + 2 layout.
-   Tablet: 2-column grid.
-   Mobile: single-column cards with full-width CTA.
-   Keep product names and CTA labels visible without hover.
-   Ensure the enquiry form is easy to complete on mobile.

### Motion

-   Subtle fade/slide-in on section entry.
-   Small card lift or image zoom on hover.
-   Smooth anchor scrolling.
-   Respect reduced-motion preferences.
-   Avoid heavy parallax, autoplay video and distracting animated
    backgrounds.

### Accessibility

-   Semantic heading order.
-   Keyboard-accessible navigation and forms.
-   Visible focus states.
-   Descriptive alt text for product images.
-   Sufficient color contrast.
-   Labels associated with all form inputs.
-   Do not rely on color alone to communicate state.

## 6. Technical implementation checklist

-   Build reusable components: `Navbar`, `Hero`, `TrustStrip`,
    `ProductCategoryCard`, `ProductDetail`, `Capabilities`,
    `ProcessSteps`, `QualitySection`, `FAQ`, `EnquiryForm`, `Footer`.
-   Keep product/category content in structured data rather than
    hardcoding repeated markup.
-   Use optimized WebP/AVIF images with sensible dimensions.
-   Lazy-load below-the-fold images.
-   Set page title, meta description, Open Graph image and favicon.
-   Add descriptive URLs/anchors and clear button labels.
-   Validate all external links and contact details.
-   Add analytics only with client approval and appropriate privacy
    notice.
-   Test desktop, tablet and mobile.
-   Test keyboard navigation, form validation, success/error states and
    spam protection.
-   Confirm hosting, domain, form backend and maintenance ownership with
    the client.

## 8. Client questions to resolve before final copy

1.  Does TSI manufacture products in-house, source and supply them, or
    do both depending on the product?
2.  Which products are currently available, and which are discontinued
    or made to order?
3.  Which products can be customized? What exactly can be changed?
4.  Does TSI provide laboratory planning, furniture, installation or
    turnkey projects?
5.  What is the exact installation, commissioning and after-sales
    support scope?
6.  What warranty applies to each product family?
7.  Can TSI share current datasheets, model lists and technical
    specifications?
8.  Can TSI provide original product photographs and
    manufacturing/testing photographs?
9.  Which certifications are current, and can the certificates be
    shared?
10. Are the brochure's experience, project and client metrics current
    and approved for publication?
11. Can customer names, logos, testimonials and project photographs be
    published?
12. What phone number, email, address, WhatsApp and social links should
    appear?
13. Who receives website enquiries, and what should happen after form
    submission?
14. Is there an approved privacy policy and consent wording?

## 9. Definition of done

The website is ready for client review when:

-   All five product families and product names are checked against the
    latest client-approved catalogue.
-   Product descriptions and technical details are verified.
-   Every company capability claim is approved.
-   All trust metrics, certifications, customer names and testimonials
    have evidence and permission.
-   Product images are authentic or clearly treated as placeholders
    during review.
-   Contact details and enquiry routing work.
-   The page is responsive, accessible and performant.
-   The client has approved the final copy and visuals.
